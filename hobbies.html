Art is something I have always done, but I didn't start to fully fall in love with it until the pandemic. When the pandemic started I started to acrylic paint, I would paint in my room for hours (hours and hours is all we had!). I then got added to an art class in highschool and I was making drawings as big as 20in by 16in. I drew in a wide variety of mediums from pencil, charcoal, oil/dry pastels, the list goes on. Art was something that turned my brain off and turned on a creative side of me. I may not have a lot of time to draw anymore but it is still something I fall back onto occasionally.
